# Talksasa Business Suite - Feature Roadmap

## Vision
Transform the payment notification system into a comprehensive business management suite for Kenyan businesses, integrating web hosting, bulk SMS, and payment processing capabilities.

---

## Current Capabilities ✅

### Payment Processing
- ✅ M-Pesa payment notifications (Paybill & Till)
- ✅ Payment tracking and analytics
- ✅ Multi-tenant architecture
- ✅ Payment history and audit trails

### SMS Services
- ✅ Talksasa Bulk SMS integration
- ✅ SMS template management
- ✅ Payment confirmation SMS
- ✅ SMS balance tracking

### Admin Features
- ✅ User management
- ✅ Admin dashboard
- ✅ Impersonation
- ✅ Multi-tenant isolation

---

## Phase 1: Enhanced Payment & Financial Management 💰

### 1.1 Advanced Payment Features
- **Payment Reconciliation**
  - Auto-match payments with invoices/orders
  - Manual reconciliation tools
  - Discrepancy alerts
  
- **Payment Analytics Dashboard**
  - Revenue trends (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly)
  - Payment method breakdown
  - Peak payment times analysis
  - Customer payment patterns
  
- **Payment Reports**
  - PDF export for accounting
  - Excel/CSV export
  - Custom date range reports
  - Tax reports (VAT, withholding tax)

### 1.2 Invoicing System
- **Invoice Generation**
  - Professional invoice templates
  - Auto-numbering
  - PDF generation
  - Email delivery
  
- **Invoice Management**
  - Create, edit, delete invoices
  - Invoice status tracking (draft, sent, paid, overdue)
  - Recurring invoices
  - Partial payments support
  
- **Payment Matching**
  - Link M-Pesa payments to invoices
  - Auto-match by reference number
  - Payment reminders

### 1.3 Financial Dashboard
- **Cash Flow Management**
  - Income vs expenses
  - Cash flow projections
  - Bank reconciliation
  
- **Financial Reports**
  - Profit & Loss statement
  - Balance sheet
  - Cash flow statement
  - Tax summaries

## Priority Recommendations

### High Priority (Q1-Q2)
1. **Enhanced Payment Analytics** - Builds on existing payment system
2. **Invoicing System** - Natural extension of payment tracking
3. **Customer Database (CRM)** - Foundation for all customer features
4. **SMS Campaign Management** - Leverages existing SMS infrastructure
5. **Web Hosting Integration** - Core business integration

### Medium Priority (Q3-Q4)
6. **E-Commerce Features** - Expands market reach
7. **Advanced Reporting** - Adds value for existing customers
8. **Automation Workflows** - Increases efficiency
9. **Customer Support System** - Improves customer satisfaction

### Low Priority (Future)
10. **Mobile Apps** - Requires separate development
11. **Advanced Analytics** - Nice-to-have features
12. **Third-Party Integrations** - As needed basis
